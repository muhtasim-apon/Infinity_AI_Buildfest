# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the International AI Builders Congress 2026 Code of Conduct
(Participants Guide section 20) in full:

- Respectful, professional conduct toward organisers, mentors, judges, volunteers,
  sponsors and fellow participants. Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination.
- All submitted work is original. AI tool usage is permitted and encouraged, and must be
  explainable and documented.
- Prohibited: plagiarism, fabricated demos, fake or simulated results presented as real,
  unauthorised copying of existing solutions.
- Legally sourced data only. Personal, health and financial data must be anonymised.
- Extra caution applies to FinSphere solutions for privacy, accuracy and public-safety
  implications.

Violations may result in removal and disqualification.
