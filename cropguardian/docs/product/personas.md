# Personas

Illustrative composites for design — not real people, not research results. Validate in interviews (Gantt 16–20 Oct).

## Rahima — smallholder rice farmer (primary)
- Grows boro and aman rice on a small plot; shared low-end Android phone; prepaid data.
- Reads Bangla slowly; prefers voice and pictures; trusts the local input dealer and neighbours.
- Pain: does not know which disease; afraid of wasting money; advice is often a product sale.
- Needs: one big button to scan; answer in simple Bangla; "what to do today"; works with weak signal.
- Design implications: icon-first UI, voice playback, ≤ 3 taps to result, offline outbox, large text.

## Karim — Sub-Assistant Agriculture Officer (secondary)
- Covers many farmers across several unions; travels by motorbike; smartphone.
- Pain: too many calls; cannot see every field; learns about outbreaks late.
- Needs: queue of uncertain cases with photos; map of reports in his area; send alerts quickly.
- Design implications: review queue with keyboard/tap shortcuts; region filter; alert approval.

## Nadia — Crop Guardian admin / agronomist lead (operator)
- Manages model versions, thresholds, KB documents; answers to partners about safety.
- Needs: one place to change τ, roll back a model, retire a KB document, and export an audit report.

## Judge (evaluation persona)
- Has minutes, not hours. Needs one-click demo, visible architecture, honest data, audit.
