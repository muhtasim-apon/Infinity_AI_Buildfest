# Usability session protocol (Raisa lead, Sara observer)

**Participants:** farmers and extension officers; informed consent in Bangla; no names/phones in notes;
no photos of faces. 20 minutes each.

**Tasks (no help given; think aloud):**
1. "Your rice leaf has spots. Use the app to find out what it might be." (success = reaches result)
2. "What should you do today, according to the app?" (success = reads/plays advice, finds dose & safety)
3. "Where did that advice come from?" (success = finds the source)
4. "Turn on alerts for your area." (success = subscription saved)

**Measures:** task success, time on task, errors, SUS questionnaire (Bangla version), quotes.
**Output:** anonymised notes in `docs/product/research-notes/` (create when sessions run) → issues in tracker.
Results are reported as observed counts (e.g. "4 of 6 completed task 1"), never extrapolated.
