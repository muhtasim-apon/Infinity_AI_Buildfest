# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the International AI Builders Congress 2026 Code of Conduct
(Participants Guide §20) in full:

- Respectful, professional conduct toward organisers, mentors, judges, volunteers, sponsors
  and fellow participants. Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination.
- All submitted work is original. AI tool usage is permitted and encouraged, and must be
  explainable and documented (`docs/congress/ai-usage-log.md`).
- Prohibited: plagiarism, fabricated demos, fake or simulated results presented as real,
  unauthorised copying of existing solutions.
- Legally sourced data only. Every dataset's licence is recorded in `docs/data/datasets.md`.
  Personal data (farmer phone numbers, locations, faces in photos) is minimised and anonymised.
- NRB / advisor contributions are transparent and listed; advisors may not influence judging.
- Respect demo time limits. Judges' decisions are final.

## Specific to agriculture
Wrong advice can destroy a harvest or poison a person. The team commits to:
- never present a guess as a diagnosis (abstain below threshold, ADR 0005);
- never recommend a pesticide without its cited source, dose and safety instructions;
- never recommend a product banned in Bangladesh; the KB excludes them by rule;
- label every demo screen that uses synthetic data.
